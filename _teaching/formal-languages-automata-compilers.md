## Course Overview

This course introduces the theoretical foundations of formal languages and automata, and applies them to the design of compilers.

### Topics
- Regular languages: finite automata and regular expressions
- Context-free languages: grammars and pushdown automata
- Lexical analysis and parsing
- Introduction to compiler construction
